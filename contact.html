FAQ

Questions clients ask first

How quickly can my team go live?

Most teams are live within 14 days of an agreed brief. That covers recruitment, training on your product and processes, and a supervised pilot before full go-live.

Where do your agents work?

From Cape Town, South Africa. Our agents speak clear, neutral English and work in a culture closely aligned with UK and European business practice.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We're happy to sign your NDA before you share any details, and we never publish client names, data or results without written permission.

How do you protect our customer data?

We work to POPIA in South Africa and are GDPR-ready for UK and European clients. Access to your systems is limited to the agents who need it, and we follow your security policies.

How is pricing structured?

We quote per agent, per month, based on your channels, hours and volumes. South African clients can be billed in Rand. Try the savings estimator for a ballpark.

Which channels do you handle?

Voice, email, live chat, WhatsApp and social media, either as one channel or a blended team.

Customer support, lead generation and back-office teams, run from Cape Town for clients in South Africa, the UK, Europe and Australia.
